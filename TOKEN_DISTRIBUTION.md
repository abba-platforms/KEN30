# TOKEN DISTRIBUTION SPECIFICATION

## Kenya 30 Index (KEN30)

Repository: https://github.com/abba-platforms/KEN30  
Network: BNB Smart Chain (Chain ID: 56)  
Token Standard: ERC-20 Benchmark Token  
Total Supply: 10,000,000,000 KEN30  

---

# 1. PURPOSE

This document defines the complete token allocation, distribution structure, and circulating supply mechanics for KEN30.

The objective is to:

- establish transparent ownership structure
- define initial and future circulating supply
- support exchange listing requirements
- enable structured liquidity provisioning
- ensure long-term stability of the benchmark ecosystem

---

# 2. TOTAL SUPPLY

Total Supply: 10,000,000,000 KEN30

- Fully minted at contract initialization
- No additional minting permitted
- No inflationary mechanism exists
- No burn mechanism is implemented

The supply is deterministic and fixed.

---

# 3. ALLOCATION OVERVIEW

The total supply is allocated across six primary categories:

1. Treasury Reserve  
2. Market Making Allocation  
3. Exchange Liquidity Allocation  
4. Institutional Allocation  
5. Founder and Team Allocation  
6. Strategic Reserve  

---

# 4. DETAILED ALLOCATION

## 4.1 Treasury Reserve

Allocation: 4,000,000,000 KEN30 (40%)

Purpose:

- long-term protocol sustainability
- ecosystem development
- strategic deployment
- future institutional partnerships

Characteristics:

- non-circulating at initialization
- controlled release over time
- governed by internal allocation policy

---

## 4.2 Market Making Allocation

Allocation: 2,000,000,000 KEN30 (20%)

Purpose:

- liquidity provisioning
- bid/ask depth support
- spread stabilization
- NAV anchoring

Characteristics:

- deployed to approved market makers
- subject to liquidity performance requirements
- may be rotated across exchanges

This allocation is critical for maintaining functional trading markets.

---

## 4.3 Exchange Liquidity Allocation

Allocation: 1,500,000,000 KEN30 (15%)

Purpose:

- initial exchange listings
- liquidity pool seeding
- trading pair activation

Characteristics:

- deployed at listing events
- paired with USDT and/or KES
- used for order book depth or AMM pools

---

## 4.4 Institutional Allocation

Allocation: 1,000,000,000 KEN30 (10%)

Purpose:

- allocation to institutional partners
- exchange partners
- financial infrastructure participants

Characteristics:

- may include vesting or lock-up conditions
- allocated via negotiated agreements
- supports long-term ecosystem alignment

---

## 4.5 Founder and Team Allocation

Allocation: 1,000,000,000 KEN30 (10%)

Purpose:

- align long-term incentives of founders and core team
- ensure continued development and maintenance of the protocol
- support strategic execution and ecosystem growth

Vesting Structure:

- 12-month cliff
- 36-month linear vesting thereafter

Characteristics:

- no tokens released at initialization
- no immediate market access
- subject to strict vesting schedule

Restrictions:

- no discretionary early unlocks
- no use for short-term liquidity
- no participation in market making activities

This allocation ensures long-term alignment between the founding team and the stability of the KEN30 ecosystem.

---

## 4.6 Strategic Reserve

Allocation: 500,000,000 KEN30 (5%)

Purpose:

- contingency reserve
- future strategic initiatives
- emergency liquidity deployment

Characteristics:

- not part of initial circulating supply
- held for long-term flexibility
