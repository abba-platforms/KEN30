# LISTING STRATEGY

## Kenya 30 Index (KEN30)

Repository: https://github.com/abba-platforms/KEN30  
Network: BNB Smart Chain (Chain ID: 56)  
Token Standard: ERC-20 Benchmark Token  

---

# 1. PURPOSE

This document defines the execution strategy for listing KEN30 on centralized exchanges.

The objective is to:

- establish initial trading markets
- deploy liquidity in a controlled manner
- ensure stable price formation relative to NAV
- coordinate market makers and exchange infrastructure
- minimize volatility during launch phase

---

# 2. LISTING OBJECTIVES

Primary objectives:

- achieve functional trading environment on Day 1
- maintain tight spreads relative to NAV
- establish consistent liquidity depth
- enable institutional participation
- build credible market structure for future listings

# 16. LISTING ECONOMICS AND COMMERCIAL TERMS

## 16.1 Listing Fee Structure

Exchanges may require:

- fixed listing fee  
- or negotiated structure including token allocation  

Typical ranges:

- Tier-2 exchanges: $20,000 – $100,000  
- Upper mid-tier exchanges: $100,000 – $300,000  

Fees may be structured as:

- cash payment  
- token allocation  
- hybrid model  

---

## 16.2 Liquidity Deposit

Certain exchanges may require:

- refundable liquidity deposit  
- locked capital commitment  

Typical structure:

- $100,000 – $500,000 equivalent  
- held for defined period (e.g., 3–6 months)  

Purpose:

- ensure sufficient liquidity  
- support market stability  

---

## 16.3 Market Maker Requirement

Exchanges may require:

- designated market maker at listing  
- proof of liquidity capability  

Minimum requirement:

- at least one approved market maker  
- preferably two independent market makers  

---

## 16.4 Fee Optimization Strategy

Negotiation considerations:

- reduce cash fees in exchange for token allocation  
- align incentives with exchange liquidity goals  
- prioritize liquidity over listing cost  

---

# 17. MARKET MAKER AGREEMENT STRUCTURE

## 17.1 Engagement Model

Market makers may be engaged under:

- non-exclusive agreements (preferred)  
- multiple market maker structure  

Avoid:

- single-point dependency  

---

## 17.2 Capital Commitment

Each market maker MUST commit:

- minimum liquidity capital per pair  
- defined inventory allocation  

Typical commitment:

- $150,000 – $500,000 per market maker  

---

## 17.3 Performance Obligations

Market makers MUST meet:

- maximum spread thresholds  
- minimum uptime requirements (e.g., 95%+)  
- minimum order book depth  

Measured continuously.

---

## 17.4 Incentive Structure

Compensation may include:

- token allocation  
- revenue sharing  
- exchange rebates  

---

## 17.5 Replacement Policy

If a market maker fails:

- performance below threshold  
- insufficient liquidity provision  

Actions:

- reduce allocation  
- replace market maker  
- reassign liquidity  

---

# 18. DAY 1 ORDER BOOK CONFIGURATION

## 18.1 Initial Pricing

Opening price MUST align with:

NAV at listing time  

---

## 18.2 Order Book Structure

Minimum required structure:

- 5–10 levels on both bid and ask sides  

---

## 18.3 Depth Distribution Example

Level 1:

- tight spread  
- $10,000 – $25,000 equivalent  

Levels 2–5:

- increasing size  
- widening spreads  

Total depth target:

- $100,000 – $300,000 within ±2% of NAV  

---

## 18.4 Spread at Launch

Initial spread:

- 0.50% – 1.50%  

Adjusted dynamically after trading begins.

---

## 18.5 Liquidity Behavior

Market makers MUST:

- maintain continuous presence  
- refresh orders rapidly  
- prevent empty order book conditions  

---

## 18.6 First Trade Handling

Objective:

- avoid extreme price spike or drop  

Approach:

- controlled liquidity  
- balanced bid/ask placement  
- gradual trade flow absorption  

---

# 19. POST-LISTING SUPPLY CONTROL

## 19.1 Early-Phase Supply Discipline

During first 30–90 days:

- no large token releases  
- no treasury selling  
- no institutional unlocks  

---

## 19.2 Treasury Policy

Treasury MUST:

- avoid active selling during early phase  
- act only as stability backstop if required  

---

## 19.3 Founder and Team Restrictions

Founder and team tokens:

- remain locked under vesting schedule  
- zero contribution to circulating supply at launch  

---

## 19.4 Institutional Allocation Control

Institutional tokens:

- subject to lock-up agreements  
- released gradually if applicable  

---

## 19.5 Market Maker Inventory Source

Market makers MUST source inventory from:

- designated market making allocation  
- NOT from open market accumulation during early phase  

---

## 19.6 Supply Shock Prevention

Mechanisms:

- controlled release schedule  
- monitoring of large transfers  
- coordination between treasury and market makers  

---

## 19.7 Stability Objective

Maintain:

- predictable circulating supply  
- controlled liquidity environment  
- minimized volatility during early trading phase
