# COMPLIANCE FRAMEWORK

## Kenya 30 Index (KEN30)

This document defines the compliance architecture, regulatory positioning, governance controls, and operational standards of the Kenya 30 Index (KEN30). It is structured to support engagement with regulators, central banks, securities exchanges, investment banks, asset managers, and institutional counterparties.

The framework establishes a comprehensive compliance posture across legal, operational, technological, and governance domains.

---

# 1. COMPLIANCE OBJECTIVE

The objective of this framework is to ensure that KEN30 operates within clearly defined legal, regulatory, and institutional boundaries while maintaining transparency, accountability, and operational integrity.

KEN30 is designed to:

- operate as a transparent benchmark infrastructure layer  
- maintain clear separation from regulated financial instruments  
- support institutional engagement through structured compliance controls  
- enable interoperability with regulated financial systems  

The framework does not assume universal regulatory acceptance but is structured to support engagement across jurisdictions.

---

# 2. PRODUCT CLASSIFICATION

KEN30 is classified as a blockchain-native benchmark index designed to represent the aggregate performance of selected equities listed on the Nairobi Securities Exchange.

KEN30 is explicitly structured as:

- a synthetic benchmark representation  
- a non-custodial digital instrument  
- a non-equity, non-debt construct  

KEN30 does not constitute:

- ownership of underlying securities  
- participation in a collective investment scheme  
- an exchange-traded fund (ETF)  
- a derivative contract with enforceable payoff obligations  

The classification is intentionally designed to distinguish KEN30 from regulated securities and investment products.

---

# 3. REGULATORY POSITIONING

KEN30 is positioned as a benchmark infrastructure layer rather than a financial instrument.

Its function is limited to:

- representing market performance  
- enabling price discovery  
- supporting integration into trading and financial systems  

KEN30 does not:

- issue securities  
- manage investment capital  
- provide financial intermediation  

Regulatory interpretation of KEN30 may vary across jurisdictions, and engagement with regulators is conducted on a case-by-case basis.

---

# 4. JURISDICTIONAL FRAMEWORK

KEN30 is globally accessible through blockchain infrastructure but must be interpreted within the legal and regulatory context of each jurisdiction in which it is accessed or utilized.

Relevant considerations include:

- local securities laws  
- digital asset regulations  
- cross-border financial restrictions  
- capital controls  

Participants are solely responsible for ensuring compliance with applicable laws within their jurisdiction.

# 5. KENYA-SPECIFIC CONSIDERATIONS

KEN30 references equities listed on the Nairobi Securities Exchange and therefore operates in proximity to Kenya’s capital markets ecosystem.

Relevant authorities include:

- Capital Markets Authority (CMA) Kenya  
- Central Bank of Kenya (CBK)  

Specific risks include:

- regulatory classification changes  
- limitations on digital asset participation  
- foreign exchange restrictions relating to the Kenyan Shilling (KES)  
- policy developments affecting capital markets  

KEN30 does not claim affiliation with or endorsement by any Kenyan regulatory authority.

---

# 6. NON-CUSTODIAL STRUCTURE

KEN30 operates as a non-custodial protocol.

At no point does the protocol:

- take custody of user funds  
- hold assets on behalf of participants  
- manage private keys  

All asset control remains with the user or the exchange platform through which trading occurs.

This structure reduces custodial risk but transfers full responsibility to participants.

---

# 7. NO FUND MANAGEMENT ACTIVITY

KEN30 does not engage in any form of fund management activity.

Specifically, KEN30 does not:

- pool capital from multiple participants  
- allocate assets across investments  
- rebalance portfolios on behalf of users  
- operate discretionary or non-discretionary mandates  

The index is a representation layer only.

---

# 8. NO INVESTMENT ADVISORY FUNCTION

KEN30 does not provide:

- investment advice

- financial recommendations  

All interaction with KEN30 is self-directed.

# 25. CROSS-BORDER COMPLIANCE
