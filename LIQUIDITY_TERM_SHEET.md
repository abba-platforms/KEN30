# 1. PURPOSE

This Liquidity Term Sheet defines the commercial, operational, and performance terms governing liquidity provision and market making for KEN30 across centralized exchange environments.

This document serves as a non-binding framework unless otherwise incorporated into a formal Market Making Agreement.

---

# 2. PARTIES

Issuer:  
Kenya 30 Index (KEN30)  
Abba Industries Inc.

Liquidity Provider(s):  
Designated Market Maker(s) (each, a “Market Maker”)

---

# 3. SCOPE

The Market Maker agrees to:

- provide continuous two-sided liquidity  
- maintain structured order book depth  
- support stable price discovery aligned with NAV  
- participate in launch and post-listing liquidity operations  

---

# 4. MARKETS

Primary trading pairs:

- KEN30 / USDT  
- KEN30 / KES  

Additional pairs may be introduced subject to mutual agreement.

---

# 5. CAPITAL COMMITMENT

## 5.1 Minimum Commitment

Each Market Maker MUST deploy:

- Minimum: $150,000 equivalent per pair  
- Target: $250,000 – $500,000 per pair  

---

## 5.2 Deployment Structure

Capital must be allocated as:

- 50% KEN30 inventory  
- 50% quote asset (USDT or KES)  

---

## 5.3 Scaling

Liquidity may be increased based on:

- volume growth  
- exchange expansion  
- institutional participation  

---

# 6. PERFORMANCE OBLIGATIONS

## 6.1 Spread

Market Maker MUST maintain:

- Target spread: ≤ 1.00%  
- Maximum spread: 2.00%  

---

## 6.2 Order Book Depth

Within ±2% of NAV:

- Minimum depth: $100,000  
- Target depth: $200,000 – $300,000  

---

## 6.3 Uptime

Market Maker MUST maintain:

- ≥ 95% quoting uptime  

---

## 6.4 Response Time

- rapid order refresh  
- no prolonged empty order book conditions  

---

# 7. NAV ALIGNMENT

Market Maker MUST:
